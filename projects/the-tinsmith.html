Overview

Two joined Trachte steel warehouses under barrel-vaulted roofs, converted instead of demolished.

Trachte was the Madison company that patented the prefabricated steel building. The warehouses are now The Tinsmith, a wedding and event venue with a glass solarium courtyard.
