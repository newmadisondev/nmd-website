## Company
New Madison Development (NMD) is a Madison, Wisconsin development company led by partners William Butcher and Eric Welch. NMD builds new housing and gives older industrial buildings new uses. On its two apartment projects NMD is joint venture partner with Stone House Development.

Headline options (declarative, short, with a period): "Madison, built forward." / "New housing. Second lives for old buildings."

Stats band (all verified): 5 projects · 256 new apartments · about 27,000 SF of industrial buildings reused · 2 joint ventures with Stone House Development

## Projects (in this order; all Completed)

### Starkweather Place
215 N Fair Oaks Avenue, Madison (east side). Multifamily, new construction. NMD with Stone House Development.
- 112 apartments, four stories, on 4.33 acres.
- Site put under contract in 2020 in the Town of Blooming Grove, annexed into Madison, rezoned November 2022.
- Underground parking, rooftop solar, dog park, canoe and kayak storage, and 2.5 acres of restored Starkweather Creek frontage.
- Designed by Potter Lawson. Built by McGann Construction.
- Opened November 1, 2024. 95% leased by mid-2026.
- Website: starkweatherplace.com
Photos: photos/starkweather-place-rendering.jpg (rendering; a drone photo may be added later as photos/starkweather-drone.jpg)

### Trader's Way
6624 Old Sauk Road, Madison (west side). Multifamily, new construction. NMD with Stone House Development.
- 144 apartments in three stories on a four-acre former farmstead, put under contract in 2023.
- Rezoning approved unanimously by the Plan Commission, then by the City Council.
- Outdoor pool, hot tub, sauna, coworking space, green roofs, underground parking with EV charging.
- Designed by Potter Lawson. Built by Stevens Construction.
- Opened September 15, 2026.
Photo: photos/traders-way-rendering.jpg

### The Tinsmith
828 E Main Street, Madison. Adaptive reuse. NMD project, William Butcher and Eric Welch.
- Two joined Trachte steel warehouses, about 9,000 SF under barrel-vaulted roofs. Trachte was the Madison company that patented the prefabricated steel building.
- Acquired in 2018 after a seven-year pursuit.
- Converted, instead of demolished, into The Tinsmith, a wedding and event venue with a glass solarium courtyard. Opened 2020.
- Adaptive Reuse Project of the Year 2021, Madison Trust for Historic Preservation. Voted best venue by Wisconsin Bride four years running.
Photo: photos/tinsmith-solarium.jpg

### 210 S Dickinson Street — Stephan & Brady
210 S Dickinson Street, Marquette neighborhood, Madison. Adaptive reuse, build-to-suit office. NMD project.
- A 1967 masonry building, built as an ice house and for decades the shop of Madison Window Cleaning.
- Acquired in 2019.
- Gutted and rebuilt as a 6,158 SF single-tenant creative office, build-to-suit for Stephan & Brady, the marketing agency, on a long-term lease. Delivered 2020. Stephan & Brady remains the tenant.
Photo: photos/dickinson-before-after.jpg (four before/after views; crop into before and after pairs)

### Northern Court
1401 and 1413 Northern Court, off East Washington Avenue, Madison. Industrial reuse. NMD project, William Butcher and Eric Welch.
- Two industrial buildings, about 12,000 SF of leasable space, converted in 2016–2017.
- Tenants: State Line Distillery (opened 2017); Imaginary Factory, a liqueur and bitters maker with a cocktail bar (about 2,500 SF; its founder was a 2026 James Beard Award finalist); Creative Energy Designs (about 3,600 SF).
- The buildings turned a short industrial street into one of Madison's craft-beverage blocks.
Photos: photos/northern-court-state-line-patio.jpg, photos/northern-court-imaginary-factory-interior.jpg
