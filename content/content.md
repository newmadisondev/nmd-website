## Company

New Madison Development is a Madison, Wisconsin real estate development company led by partners William Butcher and Eric Welch. NMD develops new housing and gives older industrial buildings new uses, often in joint venture with Stone House Development.

## Projects (in this order)

### 1. New Class A East Madison
Label to use on the site: "New Class A East Madison". Do not show the street address or the property's name.
Type: Multifamily, new construction. Joint venture with Stone House Development.
Facts:
- 112 apartments, four stories, on a 4.3-acre east-side site.
- Site put under contract in 2020, annexed into the City of Madison, rezoned in November 2022.
- Underground parking, rooftop solar, dog park, canoe and kayak storage, and 2.5 acres of restored creek frontage.
- Designed by Potter Lawson. Built by McGann Construction.
- Opened November 2024.
Photo: photos/starkweather-place-rendering.jpg (architect's rendering)

### 2. Trader's Way
Location: 6624 Old Sauk Road, Madison (west side).
Type: Multifamily, new construction. Joint venture with Stone House Development.
Facts:
- A four-acre former farmstead, put under contract in 2023.
- Rezoning approved unanimously by the Plan Commission, then by the City Council.
- 144 apartments in three stories.
- Outdoor pool, hot tub, sauna, coworking space, green roofs, and underground parking with EV charging.
- Designed by Potter Lawson. Built by Stevens Construction.
- Opened September 2026.
Photo: photos/traders-way-rendering.jpg (rendering)

### 3. The Tinsmith
Location: 828 E Main Street, Madison.
Type: Adaptive reuse.
Facts:
- Two joined Trachte steel warehouses, about 9,000 square feet under barrel-vaulted roofs. Trachte was the Madison company that patented the prefabricated steel building.
- William Butcher led the acquisition, which closed in 2018 after a seven-year pursuit.
- Instead of demolition, the buildings became a wedding and event venue with a glass solarium courtyard, opened in 2020.
- Named Adaptive Reuse Project of the Year 2021 by the Madison Trust for Historic Preservation.
Photo: photos/tinsmith-solarium.jpg (the solarium set for a dinner)

### 4. 210 S Dickinson Street — Stephan & Brady
Location: 210 S Dickinson Street, Marquette neighborhood, Madison.
Type: Adaptive reuse, build-to-suit office.
Facts:
- A 1967 masonry building, built as an ice house and for decades the shop of Madison Window Cleaning.
- Acquired in 2019 by an ownership group including NMD partner Eric Welch.
- Gutted and rebuilt as a 6,158-square-foot single-tenant creative office for Stephan & Brady, the marketing agency, delivered in 2020. Stephan & Brady remains the tenant.
Photo: photos/dickinson-before-after.jpg (four before/after views; can be cropped into separate before and after images)

### 5. Northern Court
Location: 1401–1413 Northern Court, off East Washington Avenue, Madison.
Type: Industrial reuse, craft beverage.
Facts:
- Older industrial buildings on a short street off East Washington Avenue, converted in 2016–2017 for craft-beverage tenants.
- State Line Distillery opened there in 2017.
- Imaginary Factory, a liqueur and bitters maker with a cocktail bar, built out its space there.
- Northern Court has become a small craft-beverage block on Madison's east side.
Photos: photos/northern-court-state-line-patio.jpg (State Line Distillery patio), photos/northern-court-imaginary-factory-interior.jpg (Imaginary Factory interior)
