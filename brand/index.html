Brand sheet

New Madison Development.

One page for anyone making something with the NMD name on it: the logo, the colors, the type and the tone.

01 — Logo

The NMD logo is the line-art capitol dome over NEW MADISON DEVELOPMENT, with water lines. It comes as a square frame and a circle and is always used exactly as drawn. The only permitted change is removing the white background (black on transparent), or the same artwork in white for photos and dark backgrounds.

  • Do use the square logo top left in headers; the circle centered over hero photos, on signage and as a social avatar.
  • Do keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the word MADISON, and keep it at least 64px (screen) or 0.75 in (print) tall.
  • Don’t redraw, retype, recolor (other than black or white), stretch, crop, outline or add effects.

03 — Type

Headlines and text · Hanken Grotesk

Featured projects.

Small uppercase labels · letter-spaced 0.2em

Headlines in Light (300), sentence case, short and declarative, ending with a period. Body text in Regular. Navigation and labels in small uppercase.

Statement moments · Instrument Serif

Second lives for old buildings.

Used rarely: one large centered statement over a photo. Both fonts are free Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License), self-hosted in assets/fonts.

04 — Photography

Real NMD projects and their settings only: aerials, finished interiors, exteriors and honest before-and-after pairs. Full-bleed and unfiltered. Renderings are credited (“Rendering: Potter Lawson”).

05 — Voice

Plain, specific and confident. Lead with facts: apartments, square feet, years, names. No superlatives. No construction costs, acquisition prices, financing, sale prices, valuations, rents or loan information.
